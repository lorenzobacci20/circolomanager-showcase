Who has the court this afternoon?

A member wants an hour on court. The desk diary has a free slot, but the coach keeps lesson times in a separate file. Someone has to check both before answering.

CircoloManager brings lessons, tournament matches and casual bookings onto one court schedule. From the same workspace, staff can look up a member, check a renewal and see which fees have been recorded.

Product walkthrough

Two jobs at the front desk

  1. Renew a membership

    Find a member by name, open their record and preview the new term. Check the dates and fee, confirm the renewal, then return to the member’s record to see the result.

  2. Book a court

    Open the calendar and start a booking for the centre court. Select a player and an available time, review the fee and confirm. The calendar shows the updated schedule.
